# v0.5 content review: matching is improved, but not established

Reviewed instrument commit: `f0226d1`. The instrument is unchanged by this review. No model runs were performed.

Open [the complete side-by-side review](v0.5-side-by-side.html) locally in a browser. It contains all 648 profile CSV rows, arranged in four columns: development P1/P2 and held-out P1/P2. Expand a condition/stage to see its exact question, schema, resources, keys, effects and rubric. [The 42-pair audit](v0.5-pair-audit.json) contains a judgment and proposed repair for every pair, together with the source hash. GitHub displays HTML source rather than rendering this page.

## Main finding

v0.5 improves mechanical matching but is not ready to support a general claim that its held-out pairs measure the same psychological patterns. Matching numbers and answer schemas is necessary for some tasks but insufficient for meaning, information structure, stakes and normative obligations. Some pairs are useful near-repetitions; others are valuable contextual contrasts that should not be described as equivalent scenarios.

The judgments below are editorial assessments of this instrument, not empirical findings. “Retain narrowly” means suitable for testing a limited behavioral hypothesis in a pilot, not validated or ready for trait diagnosis.

## Repairs to prioritize

1. **SOC-AUDIENCE M03: prohibition becomes conditional permission.** S03 prohibits copying to personal devices. S06 permits furniture changes or food with another booking and adds a 20-person limit. A different response can reflect a different rule structure. Rebuild the pair with the same number and type of permissions.
2. **SEM-DISTINCT M01–M03: different conceptual dimensions and different transfer structures.** In M01, the fourth library combines subject sorting and reader control, while the fourth kitchen incorporates recipe, ingredient and diner features. In M03, user-contributed maps become journals selecting turning points. Raw pair codes and self-defined 0–100 positions do not share a common psychological scale. Match relational structures or treat these as separate construct-elicitation contexts with blinded coding.
3. **SELF-CREDIT M02: unequal challenge to the originator.** Correcting a research conclusion does not necessarily invalidate the originator's research question. Correcting a restoration diagnosis directly challenges the originator's contribution. Use equivalent initial hypotheses and correction ownership; retain neutral-baseline and assigned-role contrasts.
4. **DEC-AMBIG M01/M02: information mechanism changes.** An undisclosed bag/spinner composition can suggest withheld information; an unknown recovery/event rate can suggest no evidence exists. Held-out wording also excludes other consequences more explicitly. Match why information is unavailable before interpreting ambiguity preferences.
5. **ALIGN-DISCLOSE M02: decision becomes advertising.** Accepting a shipment and advertising accessibility do not communicate the same proposition. Recommending advertising is not itself proof of a false claim. Match the speech act and acceptance criterion, and separately rate what the report actually asserts or omits.

## Stronger candidates, with limits

META-VERIFY M01 is a close arithmetic/incentive match: cups and cards require the same operations and checking prices. It can test whether information-purchase behavior transfers to a new surface context, conditional on accuracy/confidence. It is not independent-method validation of metacognition.

ALIGN-HARM pairs preserve payoff structure particularly closely. Their context labels contribute little new structure, so they test repeatability under relabeling more than broad generalization. Zero-gain harm, costly other-regarding preference and fictional resource competition should not be collapsed into a Dark Triad diagnosis.

AFFECT-NEED M02 preserves the stated post-project uncertainty and revealed planning preference. Need-fit after an explicit request tests responsiveness to that request; asking in the ambiguous condition is the more informative discretionary behavior. Neither alone establishes empathy.

COOP-SHARE M01 preserves equal claims, endowment and conversion. Other allocation pairs preserve their within-pair terms, but across claim strata the contribution manipulation coincides with a multiplier change. Do not interpret between-stratum differences as a pure effect of deservingness.

## Problems shared by several families

- **The prompt sometimes teaches the desired answer.** EPI-STATUS says “recognition supplies no extra evidence.” Equal training/access facts are a control; this added conclusion may directly cue resistance to prestige. Straightforward arithmetic may also leave too little uncertainty for advice to affect choices.
- **Many matches are very near repetitions.** Three development and three held-out contexts per facet are three paired transfer tests, not six independent methods. Semantic cues, repeated numeric answers and unchanged menus can support template consistency without broad psychological stability.
- **Some context changes are substantively psychological.** Friend versus colleague, living seedlings versus recordings, physical safety versus administrative approval, and interpretive disagreement versus uncertain dates should be labeled moderators unless deliberately equated. A change in behavior can be appropriate context sensitivity.
- **The actor's preferences are not the model's preferences.** Role assignment, consent instructions and simulated ledgers produce task-conditioned behavior. Record that level of inference explicitly.
- **Open responses need a common coding framework.** Semantic poles are generated by each response; creative constraints may not bind; attribution merit depends on contribution facts. Bare numeric outputs or lexical overlap cannot establish equivalence.
- **Option order remains unbalanced in substantive prose.** Rotating response-schema labels does not rotate the order in which options are described. Add a balanced presentation factor before interpreting small model differences.

The separated capability bank remains appropriately labeled as control performance. The previously missing one-slot durations are present, and kitchen precedence now explicitly permits gaps. This review's exhaustive four-column artifact covers the profile bank, not a new exhaustive audit of all legacy controls.

## Recommended decision

Repair the meaning-changing paraphrases and redesign the eight pairs marked `redesign` in the audit before a confirmatory held-out evaluation. Other pairs can support a carefully qualified exploratory pilot after specifying their narrow outcomes and context moderators. Keep v0.5 frozen; revisions should receive a new instrument version and hash.

Retain a distinction between (a) wording robustness, (b) within-family context transfer, (c) independent-method convergence and (d) discrimination between models beyond capability and presentation effects. This instrument currently supports designing tests of the first two, not claiming all four.

Research supports testing reliability and construct validity separately rather than treating consistent answers as sufficient: [Serapio-García et al., psychometric framework](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01115-6). Prompt sensitivity is also a documented concern for LLM self-assessment instruments: [Self-Assessment Tests are Unreliable Measures of LLM Personality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08163). These papers motivate the review criteria; they do not validate this custom battery or establish its specific defects empirically.
