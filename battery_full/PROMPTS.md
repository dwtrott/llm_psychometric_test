# Expanded psychological prompt battery

240 conversation cards / 280 question stages, 11 categories and 22 facets. Every card starts a fresh conversation. Follow-ups stay in that conversation. 120 cards are development and 120 held-out. The two wordings share identical facts; only task instructions are paraphrased. These are original exploratory probes, not a validated full personality inventory. See [design and running guide](README.md).
