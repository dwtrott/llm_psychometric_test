# Affective appraisal (AFFECT)

[Back to all questions](../QUESTIONS.md)

Researcher scoring note: Agreement with goal- and knowledge-based appraisal key.

Interpretation limit: Tests understanding of affective situations, not experienced emotion or empathy.
