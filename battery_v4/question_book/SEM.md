## SEM-LINK: Contextual conceptual associations

Estimate each cue distribution across scenario forms, wording and audience. Preserve common and context-specific relationships.

Alternative explanations: Lexical habits and topical priming can identify a model without a psychological explanation. Do not merge synonyms automatically.
